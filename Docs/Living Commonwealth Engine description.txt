Living Commonwealth Engine (LCE)

Building living worlds through simulation.

Living Commonwealth Engine (LCE) is an open-source modern C++23 simulation framework designed to bring persistent life, memory, relationships, and emergent behaviour to Bethesda Game Studios titles.

Rather than scripting every interaction, LCE provides systems that simulate entities, locations, groups, memories, relationships, events, and world progression through modular services.

The first supported platform is Fallout 4 through CommonLibF4 and F4SE. The engine itself remains platform independent and is designed to support future platform adapters without changing the core architecture.

LCE is built with three primary goals:

• Create believable living worlds.
• Provide a stable SDK for developers.
• Teach through code.

Every subsystem is designed to be documented, understandable, maintainable, and extensible.
